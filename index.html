Our Philosophy

Cooking
with Intention

At Zaffron, we believe that great food begins long before it reaches the kitchen. Our spices are sourced directly from Wayanad's highland estates. Our seafood comes fresh from the fishermen of Fort Kochi each morning. Our vegetables are grown organically just beyond the city.

Every element on your plate has been chosen with deliberate care — because we believe you can taste the difference between food that was rushed and food that was loved.

Chef Rajan
Founder · Head Chef · Zaffron
